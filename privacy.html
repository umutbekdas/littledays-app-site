Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

LittleDays Baby is published by Umut Bekdaş, an individual developer. This policy explains what happens to information you put in the iPhone app and information you choose to send to support.

Information you put in the app

You can record a child’s profile, care and health observations, tasks, reminders, notes, memories and supplies. You may add photos, videos, audio or documents. The app stores these records and original media on your iPhone. It does not require a LittleDays account or send your records to a LittleDays-operated server. It does not use your records for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Optional iCloud backup and exports

If you turn on private iCloud backup, the app copies records and registered original media to the private CloudKit database of your Apple account. Apple handles that storage under its own terms and privacy policy; the developer does not have access to your private CloudKit records. You can turn off future app backups and separately request deletion of the app-managed private iCloud backups in the app.

If you make a portable full backup, the app encrypts the archive. Keep its recovery key and exported file safe; the developer does not keep a copy. When you export or share a file, the destination you choose receives it under that destination’s privacy practices.

Permissions

The app asks for camera, photo-library or microphone access when you use a related feature. It asks for notification permission when you set up reminders. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings. Local reminders are delivered by iOS and are not a record that care was given.

Purchases

Apple processes the annual subscription and its free introductory offer through the App Store. The app checks your Apple purchase entitlement to decide whether editing is available. The developer does not receive your payment-card details through the app. Without an active subscription, existing records remain readable and exportable.

If you contact support

If you email supportlittledays@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and whatever you include. Support correspondence is handled through Gmail (Google), separately from records stored in the app. We use it to reply and investigate your request. Please avoid sending a child’s photos, recordings or health details unless necessary. Support correspondence is deleted from the support mailbox within 12 months after the request closes, unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. You may ask for earlier deletion using that address; we will explain if a legal obligation prevents it.

Deleting information

The app offers separate controls to erase local app-managed data and delete its private iCloud backups. Erasing local data does not erase files you exported, Apple device backups or private iCloud backups. Another device with backup enabled may upload its records again. If you need help with a deletion request, contact the address above.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app’s data handling changes. The current version and effective date will be shown here. For privacy questions, email supportlittledays@gmail.com.
